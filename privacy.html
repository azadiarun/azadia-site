Azadia Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Azadia is a running training app. It is built so that your data stays on your iPhone. We do not run servers that receive your information, and we do not collect, sell, or share any personal data.

Health data

With your permission, Azadia reads the following from Apple Health: workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, VO₂ max, and sleep. It uses this data only to build and adapt your training plan and to show your progress inside the app.

  • Azadia only reads Health data. It does not write to Apple Health.
  • Health data is processed on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.
  • Health data is not used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is not stored in iCloud by Azadia.
  • You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app (Profile → Apps → Azadia) or in iPhone Settings.

Information you enter

Your training profile (goal race, schedule, injuries, settings, shoes) and your plan are saved only on your device. If you choose to export a backup, the file goes wherever you send it (for example Files or AirDrop); it contains your profile and settings, not your Health data.

Plan Assistant

The Plan Assistant uses Apple's on-device language model. Your messages and training data are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to Azadia or any external AI service.

No tracking or analytics

Azadia contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data Azadia stored. Your Apple Health data is managed by Apple Health and is not affected.

Children

Azadia is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
